This document describes what information the Hydrant Map app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

What the app collects

The app is designed to collect as little information as possible. The following lists every form of data the app touches.

  • Your approximate location — only when you tap the GPS button (📍) or grant location permission. The location is used inside your browser to center the map and find nearby hydrants; it is not transmitted to any server controlled by the app's operator.
  • Address you type or paste — sent to Google's Geocoding API so it can be converted to map coordinates. Google's privacy policy applies to this lookup.
  • Your clipboard content — read by the app on launch only when you have already granted clipboard permission, and only used to detect a NowForce-style fire dispatch ("Nearest Box: NNNN" / address). Clipboard content is never stored or transmitted.
  • Local cache — Boston hydrant, fire-box, fire-district, building, and running-card data are downloaded once and stored in your browser's IndexedDB so the app works offline. You can clear it any time via your browser's site-settings page.

The app does not have its own backend server, log files, analytics, or user accounts. The author cannot see who is using the app or what addresses have been searched.

Cookies & advertising

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. When AdSense is active:

  • Google may set cookies in your browser to serve ads relevant to your interests and to measure ad performance.
  • Google may collect information including (but not limited to) your IP address, device type, browser, and pages visited.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google's Ads Settings page or by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

Google's full privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-party services

The app uses these external services. Each has its own privacy policy linked below.

  • Google Maps Platform — map tiles, address geocoding. policies.google.com/privacy
  • City of Boston GIS Portal — public hydrant, fire-box, and fire-district datasets. Used as a fallback if the offline bundle hasn't downloaded yet. boston.gov privacy statement
  • Boston Planning & Development Agency — public building-footprint data.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static files the app is served from. GitHub may log connection metadata (IP, user-agent). GitHub privacy statement
  • Google AdSense (when ads are present) — advertising network. See above.

Children

The app is intended for adult users (firefighters and members of the public looking up hydrant locations). It does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Your rights

  • Location: revoke the app's location permission at any time in your browser or device settings.
  • Clipboard: revoke clipboard permission at any time. The app falls back to manual paste.
  • Cached data: clear it any time via your browser's site-settings page (look for "Clear data" or "Site storage").
  • EU/UK residents: under GDPR you may request deletion of any personal data the operator holds. Because the app does not operate a backend, the operator does not hold personal data; the only mitigation available is at the third-party services listed above (e.g., Google Ads Settings).
  • California residents: under CCPA you have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Same caveat applies — the operator does not hold any.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when new features are added or third-party services change. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. There is no email notification for changes — please re-read periodically.
