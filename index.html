🚒 Hydrant Map

Boston Fire Department

A quick-reference tool for finding the nearest fire hydrants to any Boston address. Built independently to complement NowForce dispatch.

How to use

  • From NowForce: tap Open with → BFD Hydrants
  • Manually: paste any Boston address, hit Enter
  • Current location: tap 📍
  • Tap the BOX badge to view the full running card

What it shows

  • Nearest hydrants within 500 ft, ranked & color-coded
  • Building footprint of the incident (red highlight)
  • BFD district badge (D1–D12)
  • Nearest alarm box + 1st / SS / 2nd / 3rd due companies

Data sources

  • Hydrants, fire boxes, districts: gis.boston.gov
  • Building footprints: Boston Planning & Development Agency
  • Running cards: BFD official cards
  • Map tiles & geocoding: Google Maps Platform
For reference only. Always verify hydrant status, accessibility, and dispatch information on scene. Out-of-service hydrants and recent operational changes may not be reflected.
